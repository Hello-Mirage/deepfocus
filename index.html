Features

Engineered for Flow State

Traditional blocklists are too easy to bypass. DeepFocus provides network-level protection and deep OS integration to secure your focus.

System-Wide Rust Daemon

Operates directly at the Windows network and hosts level. Blocks distracting domains completely across all web browsers, incognito modes, and desktop apps with zero workarounds.

Chrome Extension Sync

Synchronizes seamlessly with your browser. When you visit blocked domains, the companion extension intercepts the request and instantly redirects you to a beautiful distraction-free splash page.

Privacy-First & Local

No cloud databases, no tracking analytics, and no mandatory accounts. DeepFocus runs entirely locally on your hardware. Your block configurations and browsing sessions stay yours alone.

Common Questions

Have questions about how DeepFocus works under the hood?

DeepFocus operates a native Rust background daemon with administrative privileges. When you click "Start Focus" in your dashboard, the daemon updates the system routing parameters and maps the blocked domains to 127.0.0.1 (localhost) in the Windows hosts configuration. This guarantees that requests are blocked instantly before any browser processing, making it impossible to circumvent by changing browsers or going incognito.

No, but it is highly recommended! The Rust network daemon handles the absolute blocking at the IP routing level. However, visiting blocked sites on a normal browser will result in a generic browser "Site cannot be reached" error.

The optional DeepFocus Chrome Extension runs as a companion. It monitors your browser and redirects those connection failures to an elegant, custom built-in HTML splash screen that actively reminds you to get back to work, ensuring a smoother user experience.

There are no remote sync servers, tracker beacons, or tracking telemetry. Your lists, session analytics, and app configuration remain entirely sandboxed on your personal storage.

DeepFocus always cleanly reverts any changes when you stop a session or exit the dashboard. In the rare event of a system crash, simply open the dashboard and click the "Revert Changes" button in the sidebar. This will instantly communicate with the local daemon service to restore your default original configurations safely.
