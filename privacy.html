Privacy Policy for DeepFocus

Effective Date: May 14, 2026

1. Data Collection & Privacy

DeepFocus is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. All focus session data, pomodoro statistics, website blocking rules, and daily analytics are processed and stored strictly locally on your device. We do not transmit, collect, inspect, or monetize your personal data, web browsing activity, or app usage habits.

2. Desktop Permissions (runFullTrust)

DeepFocus requires desktop permissions (`runFullTrust`) solely to manage local background filtering rules for website blocking and interface with standard Windows desktop system notifications. No browsing history or network traffic ever leaves your local machine.

3. Third-Party Services

DeepFocus does not include any third-party tracking, telemetry, or advertising SDKs.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our privacy practices, please contact HavicSoftWorks / Hello-Mirage via our official GitHub repository.
